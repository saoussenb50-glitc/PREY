# Experiment #002 Decision Protocol

Precommitted on 20 September 2026 before Experiment #002 reached its checkpoint. This protocol governs the evidence recorded at **26 September 2026**, or earlier only if a defensible qualified-view metric reaches 100. It does not classify the current result.

## Frozen commercial criterion

The primary success criterion is **at least one paid manual run**: money actually received, or a platform-confirmed paid order, for the defined US$20 service. Views, upvotes, comments, DMs, questions, interest, promises, and failed payments are secondary evidence and must never be relabeled as commercial success or revenue.

At the checkpoint, classify in this order: **A**, then **B**, then **C** if positive human/payment evidence exists; otherwise **F** if the test was technically compromised; otherwise **D** if meaningful exposure is established; otherwise **E**. Stop at the first match. This makes the states mutually exclusive without allowing a later technical problem to erase a verified payment or human signal.

## Outcome states and precommitted actions

### A. PAID SIGNAL

**Evidence required:** at least one independent buyer paid, or a platform confirmed a paid order, for manual correction of an existing machine-generated English SRT covering no more than 20 source-video minutes at the stated US$20 price. A promise or failed transfer does not qualify.

**Next allowed move:** manually fulfill using `mission-14/FIRST_BUYER_RUNBOOK.md`; record payment, delivery facts, actual workflow, time, ambiguity, fees, and friction; then seek one second independent comparable paid delivery in a separately authorized market action. Reusable software remains forbidden until the Build Gate is met.

### B. BUYING INTENT, NO PAYMENT

**Evidence required:** no paid run, but at least one relevant prospective buyer reached a genuine purchase-stage action—such as asking how to start, agreeing scope, asking about turnaround or payment, offering to send the input, or attempting payment. The conversation and its fit to the offer must be recorded. Generic price curiosity without a credible purchase step is interest, not this state.

**Next allowed move:** identify the narrowest evidenced conversion friction—price, trust, scope, turnaround, payment, input readiness, or another specific block. Permit **one** controlled retest changing only that evidenced factor, with a fresh ID/window and unchanged fields documented. Do not pool results or build software.

### C. INTEREST, NO BUYING INTENT

**Evidence required:** no paid run and no purchase-stage action, but at least one relevant person meaningfully asked about the job, fit, input, output, workflow, or constraints. The signal must plausibly come from the intended buyer context; compliments, votes, and generic comments do not qualify.

**Next allowed move:** inspect the mismatch between the observed job and the offer. Form at most **one** revised, falsifiable bet only if the recorded interaction supports a specific change. Otherwise reserve or end the bet as inconclusive. Any revised exposure requires separate authorization and a new experiment; no software build.

### D. MEANINGFUL EXPOSURE, ZERO QUALIFIED INTEREST

**Evidence required:** no relevant interest, buying intent, or paid run; the experiment remained accessible and measurable enough; and the meaningful-exposure standard below is satisfied by defensible evidence that a relevant audience had a real opportunity to encounter and act on the complete offer.

**Next allowed move:** choose either a market kill of the **tested offer/segment/surface combination** or one major segment/offer revision, only where the exposure evidence shows which claim failed. Do not generalize to all subtitle services. A revision becomes a new experiment and requires separate authorization. Do not launch Experiment #001 as compensation.

### E. INSUFFICIENT EXPOSURE

**Evidence required:** no paid run or stronger human signal, no execution failure that prevented the test, and available evidence cannot defensibly establish that enough relevant humans encountered the offer. Generic Reddit views alone, including the current 58, do not establish qualified exposure.

**Next allowed move:** record **INCONCLUSIVE — DISTRIBUTION UNPROVEN**. Do not market-kill or alter the core offer. The only permitted next hypothesis is distribution: design one bounded distribution retest on a legitimate surface with a more defensible audience/exposure measure. Launch requires separate authorization.

### F. EXECUTION / MEASUREMENT FAILURE

**Evidence required:** States A–C are not met, and removal, inaccessibility, rule/moderation intervention, account/access failure, broken response path, material post corruption, or loss of essential measurement makes the intended exposure or interpretation technically unreliable. Record the failure and when it occurred. Mere absence of a qualified-view metric is not automatically failure if other evidence can establish meaningful exposure; otherwise use State E.

**Next allowed move:** record an **EXECUTION BLOCK** and the compromised interval. Do not market-kill. Preserve the offer, and permit only a fresh bounded retest that repairs the documented execution or measurement fault, subject to separate authorization.

## Meaningful exposure standard

PREY has no justified numerical qualified-view threshold for this Reddit test. Reddit's generic view count cannot reveal buyer relevance and must not be converted into qualified views.

Meaningful exposure requires a documented, credible chain showing all of the following:

1. the complete offer was continuously accessible for the intended window, with a working opt-in response path;
2. the surface or placement plausibly concentrated relevant creators or buyers, supported by direct audience evidence rather than the community name alone;
3. evidence demonstrates that multiple independent relevant humans actually encountered, opened, or engaged with the offer, or that the placement reliably delivered it to such an audience;
4. the evidence is strong enough to distinguish offer rejection from mere lack of distribution.

Possible support includes a platform metric explicitly limited to the intended audience, multiple independently attributable relevant impressions, moderator/community placement data showing actual delivery to the target cohort, or several relevant non-buying exposures whose identities need not be retained. No single generic aggregate view count, upvote count, subscriber count, or elapsed time is sufficient. If this chain is absent at the checkpoint, classify **E**, not **D**.

## Second comparable paid delivery rule

The Build Gate requires **two independent comparable paid deliveries**. The second delivery counts only when:

- it comes from a different unaffiliated paying customer, without subsidy, barter, refund-for-proof, or coordinated test behavior;
- each buyer supplies a real existing machine-generated English SRT and corresponding source audio/video they are entitled to share;
- each job substantially repeats the same paid outcome: manual correction into a valid, upload-ready SRT plus timestamped ambiguity handling;
- each job remains close enough in scope and workflow to test recurrence of the same job, including the same core listening, wording, timing/structure QA, and delivery steps;
- payment is actually received or platform-confirmed for each delivery; and
- both deliveries and their workflow evidence are recorded separately.

It does **not** count as comparable when the second item is from the same customer, free, promised but unpaid, a revision of the first order, a synthetic/internal sample, a gift or friendly test, only transcription from scratch, translation, caption styling/burn-in, unrelated editing, a materially different long-form/custom engagement, or a job whose core input/output and manual workflow differ substantially. A repeat purchase by the same buyer is useful recurrence evidence but does not satisfy the independent-customer Build Gate.
