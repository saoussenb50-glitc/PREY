## Measurement definitions

- **Qualified view:** one platform-reported view of the r/SquarePOS_Users post during the test window. Because community membership does not prove the viewer runs consignment payouts, treat this as a distribution proxy, not a known unique qualified operator. Record the platform number honestly and note this limitation.
- **Inquiry:** an inbound response through the request link or a public reply that states the person operates or manages a Square-based multi-vendor/consignment workflow and asks a fit, input, timing, or purchase question. Likes, upvotes, compliments, and generic comments do not count.
- **Payment signal:** the qualified operator explicitly accepts the US$25 scope and requests the payment method or accepts a payment request. This is intent, not revenue.
- **Actual paid run:** US$25 has cleared from an independent qualified operator for an in-scope closed-period run. A promised payment, test payment from a friend, refund, or free sample does not count.
- **Schema submission:** a qualified operator voluntarily sends column headers or an anonymized sample through the private intake path.

## Success and kill rule

- Success requires at least one actual paid manual run.
- Keep the stronger Mission 05 diagnostic targets: three qualified inquiries and two schema submissions. They reveal trust and workflow fit but do not replace payment.
- If one paid run occurs, deliver manually. Do not build software. Reusable software remains forbidden until two independent paid deliveries have substantially the same input/output.
- If three qualified prospects receive the complete US$25 offer and none pays, kill the offer.
- At the 7-day or 100-view checkpoint, zero paid runs with 100 platform-reported views is **offer failure** under this test, while recording the qualification limitation.
- If the post is removed, never permitted, or receives too few platform-reported views to reach the checkpoint in seven days, record **distribution failure**, not offer failure. Do not claim the price or need was disproved.
