# Square payout pack request

Send this by email or a private form only after the operator opts in from the public post.

## Fit check — no sensitive data

- Business type:
- Number of consignors in the next closed payout period:
- Approximate number of sale/refund lines:
- Square export/report name:
- Column headers only, or an anonymized three-row sample:
- How is each item linked to a consignor (SKU prefix, category, custom field, other)?
- Split rates used:
- Are discounts and refunds visible at item level? Yes / No / Unsure
- Should processing fees be shown only, deducted from the store share, or handled another way?
- Desired delivery date:

## Do not send

Square credentials, API keys, bank details, full card numbers, customer names/contact details, tax IDs, consignor home addresses, or unredacted data not needed for the payout calculation.

## File handoff after fit is confirmed and payment is agreed

Use a private, access-restricted upload link controlled by the shop or a mutually agreed encrypted attachment. Share the minimum required exports. Remove customer-identifying columns first. PREY confirms receipt, processes locally, returns the pack through the same restricted channel, and deletes working copies within seven days after acceptance. The shop keeps its originals and approves payouts independently.
