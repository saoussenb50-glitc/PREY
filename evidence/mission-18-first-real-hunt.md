## Candidate C-30 — Freight POD-to-invoice exception packet

**For:** a US small freight broker with carrier invoices waiting on proof of delivery.

**When:** a carrier invoice must be approved but the rate confirmation, BOL, POD, or invoice fields do not reconcile.

**They need:** a bounded document-match and exception list before approval.

**Currently:** people perform document matching manually or use freight-audit tooling. Laneproof describes matching carrier invoices against rate confirmations, BOLs, and PODs before payment; Ascent describes matching invoices to supporting shipment documents; Mirage Metrics describes inconsistent POD formats and matching before invoicing.

**PREY wedge:** one human-reviewed exception packet for ten loads; no carrier contact, payment approval, or software.

**Money evidence:** an Upwork transportation-operations service lists freight invoice audit and validation at $40, $120, and $250 tiers.

**Distribution:** relevant logistics communities exist, but this hunt did not verify a compliant zero-cost place to post this offer.

**Cheap test:** only after verifying a permitted opt-in surface, offer one manual ten-load exception packet.

**Kill condition:** no permitted surface, or no operator will provide a redacted ten-load batch.

**Classification:** RESERVE. The missing fact is a permitted zero-cost opt-in distribution surface.

- https://ascentlogistics.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Ascent_Freight_Audit_and_Pay_6.10.20.pdf

## Candidate C-31 — Amazon FBA sourcing-cost reimbursement case pack

**For:** a small FBA seller documenting a reimbursement claim.

**When:** Amazon needs sourcing-cost support for a claim.

**They need:** an organized existing-invoice packet, without PREY filing or representing the claim.

**Currently:** sellers submit documents or use commission reimbursement services. Amazon's reimbursement policy discusses approved submitted amounts or estimates; TrueOps markets commission-based reimbursement work.

**PREY wedge:** one manual sourcing-cost packet.

**Money evidence:** commission-priced reimbursement services exist.

**Distribution:** r/FulfillmentByAmazon is a seller gathering place, but a visible rule notice says service promotion needs moderator approval and otherwise results in a ban.

**Cheap test:** none until a permitted surface is verified.

**Kill condition:** policy-sensitive responsibility plus the unverified compliant distribution path.

**Classification:** KILL — DESK RESEARCH. No market judgment.

## Candidate C-32 — Shopify chargeback evidence chronology packet

**For:** a small Shopify merchant with an open dispute and scattered order, delivery, and support records.

**When:** the merchant must assemble evidence under a dispute deadline.

**They need:** a human-prepared chronology/checklist while retaining submission responsibility.

**Currently:** merchants manually collect evidence or pay chargeback-management platforms. Chargeflow advertises evidence collection/submission and charges 25% of recovered chargebacks.

**PREY wedge:** one reason-code-aware evidence chronology with no processor access or claim submission.

**Money evidence:** paid, outcome-priced chargeback management exists.

**Distribution:** r/Shopify explicitly prohibits promoting services and soliciting contact.

**Cheap test:** none until a permitted opt-in surface is verified.

**Kill condition:** no differentiated, compliant surface relative to full-service incumbents.

**Classification:** KILL — DESK RESEARCH. No market judgment.
