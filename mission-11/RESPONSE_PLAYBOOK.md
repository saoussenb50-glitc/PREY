> The test price is fixed at US$20 for up to 20 source-video minutes, so I’m keeping the price and scope unchanged.

Before we start, please confirm: the source video is 20 minutes or less; you have the matching machine-generated English SRT; you’re authorized to share the footage; and the request does not include from-scratch transcription, translation, legal/certified transcription, redaction, speaker research, project files, or publishing. Please also list any known-name spellings, censor substitutions, and your deadline. After scope is confirmed, we’ll agree on a safe permitted payment and file-transfer method before you send the files.
