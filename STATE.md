## Current diagnosis

PREY examined 29 distinct candidates across Missions 01–04, asked zero actual target users, made zero offers, and ran zero money tests. It therefore has not established that attractive tiny markets are absent. It has established that its process overuses desk research and applies a micro-SaaS-shaped competition gate too broadly.

Of 28 killed candidates, 19 (68%) were killed primarily by the incumbent/competition-gap gate. An inexpensive/free incumbent is a hard death only when it serves the same narrow user, workflow, outcome, exceptions, and adoption conditions. Otherwise it may validate demand while leaving a service, specialization, packaging, trust, speed, integration, or pricing wedge that research alone cannot resolve.
