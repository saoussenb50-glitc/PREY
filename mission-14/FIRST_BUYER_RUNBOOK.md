# First Buyer Runbook — Experiment #002

Use only after a genuine inbound buyer appears. This is a manual operational checklist, not software and not a claim of capability.

## Before work

- [ ] Verify the request is manual correction of an **existing machine-generated English SRT**, not transcription from scratch, translation, legal certification, caption burn-in, or another excluded service.
- [ ] Confirm the source video totals **20 minutes or less**.
- [ ] Confirm the buyer has the existing machine-generated SRT and is entitled to provide the necessary material.
- [ ] Ask which supplied speaker-label convention to follow; do not invent a convention if none is supplied.
- [ ] Record any supplied censorship instructions exactly; do not infer unstated censorship.
- [ ] Record known names or terms only if the buyer supplies them or the audio clearly supports them.
- [ ] Confirm the fixed price is **US$20**.
- [ ] Confirm a realistic manual turnaround without claiming an unproven speed.
- [ ] Agree a legitimate payment method before work begins. Do not exchange credentials, use shared accounts, or expose banking information.
- [ ] Receive only the necessary SRT and source file or access-controlled link. Do not request account credentials or unrelated files.
- [ ] Preserve buyer privacy: minimize retention and disclosure of buyer material, keep it private, and use it only for delivery and necessary factual records.

## Manual delivery

- [ ] Preserve a working copy of the received SRT.
- [ ] Manually compare every cue against the audible source and correct wording where supported.
- [ ] Mark uncertainty rather than guessing.
- [ ] Create a timestamped ambiguity log for unresolved speech or uncertain names/terms.
- [ ] Follow the supplied speaker-label convention where the source supports it.
- [ ] Follow the buyer's supplied censorship instructions.
- [ ] Do not insert unsupported names, terms, speakers, or facts.

## Minimum delivery QA

- [ ] Cue numbering is valid and sequential.
- [ ] Timestamps use valid SRT format and each end follows its start.
- [ ] Cues are chronological.
- [ ] No malformed or incomplete blocks remain.
- [ ] Wording has been checked against the audible source.
- [ ] Uncertain speech is marked, not guessed.
- [ ] Every ambiguity entry has a timestamp.
- [ ] Requested speaker conventions are followed where supported.
- [ ] Supplied censorship instructions are followed.
- [ ] No unsupported names or terms were inserted.
- [ ] The output opens or parses correctly where practical with available tools; do not claim compatibility that was not tested.

## Delivery and evidence

- [ ] Deliver only the corrected SRT and agreed ambiguity log through the agreed legitimate channel.
- [ ] Record a paid run, customer, and revenue only after payment is actually received or a platform confirms the paid order.
- [ ] Record gross amount and actual payment fees separately; never record credentials or sensitive payment details.
- [ ] Record manual time, repeated steps, difficult cases, input defects, buyer clarifications, QA friction, and delivery friction for comparison with a possible second independent paid delivery.
- [ ] Do not reuse buyer content as a sample or proof without explicit permission.
